I’m Rasmus Jon, a multidisciplinary creative producer working where sound, story, performance and space meet.

01 / Practice

I create structured experiences that give ideas a body — from the first spark of a concept to the final moment it meets an audience.

My practice moves between music composition, audio design, direction, film, editing, interactive worlds and live performance. I’m interested in the connections between them: how sound can shape a room, how a visual rhythm can carry emotion, and how a project becomes more powerful when every element has a reason to be there.

02 / Collaboration

I work closely with artists, collaborators and institutions to turn ambitious ideas into clear, immersive productions. The process matters as much as the outcome: building trust, making room for experimentation, and bringing different perspectives into the same direction.

Whether the format is a performance, a film, a game, an installation or a piece of music, I want the work to feel alive. Something people can enter, remember, and carry with them.
