## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory convergence of high-end Nordic cultural curation, cinematic mise-en-scène (A24), and large-scale immersive spatial design (Venice Biennale, Marshmallow Laser Feast). Centered on the guiding ethos *"I create worlds,"* the interface abandons generic software tropes in favor of an architectural, deliberate, and gallery-grade spatial narrative.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Atmospheric & Tactile:** Rich, warm off-white canvas paired with obsidian density. It feels physical, like unbleached cotton rag paper pressed against volcanic basalt.
- **Cinematic Authorial Voice:** High-tension typographic scales that frame interactive work not as portfolio "case studies," but as immersive world archives and productions.
- **Nordic Restraint meets Surreal Scale:** Composed whitespace, unhurried negative space, and laser-precise technical metadata, balanced with sweeping full-bleed media and monumental typography.

### Target Audience
Museum directors, biennial curators, luxury fashion houses, boundary-pushing directors, and institutional collaborators seeking an auteur capable of staging physical, digital, and hybridized environments.

## Colors

The color system relies on low-saturation organic tonalities punctuated by precise kinetic energy. It establishes a luminous, tactile surface rather than harsh digital white.

- **Canvas Base (`#EAE8E3`):** Uncoated raw limestone / archival paper stock. Acts as the primary backdrop for light environments.
- **Deep Obsidian (`#161514`):** The primary ink. A charcoal-black with nuanced warm brown undertones, ensuring high contrast without digital sterile harshness.
- **Vermilion Ember (`#D14D28`):** A cinematic red-amber accent. Used sparingly for interactive focal points, live exhibition cues, active timecodes, and directional indices.
- **Weathered Basalt (`#8C867E`):** A balanced stone neutral for technical taxonomy, curatorial metadata, year stamps, and architectural grid lines.
- **Deep Chamber (`#121110`):** An inverted dark surface dedicated to black-box video screenings, virtual realm viewports, and light-installation walkthroughs.

## Typography

The type system is an intentional juxtaposition of classical grandeur, refined Nordic humanism, and precise spatial notation.

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers editorial weight, high contrast, and sculptural romance. Used for evocative world titles, manifesto statements, and production chapters.
- **Narrative & Body (`Manrope`):** Unadorned, hyper-legible, geometric yet organic sans-serif. Carries curatorial essays, production background, artist statements, and collaborative credits.
- **Spatial Metadata & System Indices (`Space Grotesk`):** Fixed-width-inspired sans-serif for spatial dimensions, temporal timestamps, venue coordinates, light-rig protocols, and catalog identifiers. Always rendered in structured casings or small caps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy rejects uniform application containers and dense modular dashboards, adopting instead a cinematic architectural grid inspired by publication design and multi-screen projection mapping.

### Structural Mechanics
- **12-Column Asymmetric Fluid Grid:** Designed for deliberate imbalance. Large hero media spans 8 to 11 columns, leaving curatorial notations anchored in 2-to-3 column offsets.
- **Rhythmic Negative Space:** Expansive vertical pauses (`unit-2xl` and `unit-3xl`) allow projects to breathe, creating the cadence of moving through physical museum chambers.
- **Viewport Dynamic Framing:** Content boundaries are locked to fixed gutters and generous margins (`margin-desktop`), with select installations bursting into full-bleed horizontal scrolls or edge-to-edge ambient projections.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, multi-axis asymmetric placement, parallel side-scroll metadata paired with sticky imagery.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, reduced vertical rhythm (`unit-xl`), single horizontal rail for installation credits.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns. Narrative shifts from horizontal multi-column tension to an episodic, vertical scroll. Display sizes reduce via fluid clamp scales to preserve editorial framing without line wraps that destroy typographic poise.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids software drop-shadows, plastic blurs, and skeuomorphic embossing. Spatial dimension is evoked through physical and filmic staging:

- **Surface Tonal Planes:** Depth is achieved by placing deep obsidian visual viewports directly onto the raw chalk/canvas background. Overlapping layers simulate framed prints or spatial scrims.
- **Hairline Razor Borders:** Zero-blur, ultra-fine 1px outlines (`#161514` at 12% opacity on light surfaces; `#EAE8E3` at 16% opacity on dark chambers) segment gallery items and structural coordinates cleanly.
- **Grain & Vignette Textures:** Subtle procedural film grain overlaid across hero frames evokes analog medium-format film and physical installation projection surfaces.
- **Parallax Plane Shifts:** Minimal z-index layering where typographic folios and live index markers sit static while media slides beneath them, referencing gallery wall labeling.

## Shapes

The design system is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. 

Every perimeter, interactive trigger, image container, and curatorial frame terminates in crisp 90-degree corners. This uncompromising geometry references architectural blueprints, archival passe-partout matting, 35mm contact sheets, and physical exhibition pedestals. No rounded UI cards or pill badges are permitted.

## Components

### Action Triggers (Buttons & Interactive Links)
- **Exhibition CTA:** Sharp rectangular frame with a 1px solid obsidian border. Transparent fill with obsidian text in `label-caps`. On hover, fills instantly with `#161514` while text inverts to `#EAE8E3`.
- **Directional Monolith:** Minimalist text link accompanied by an elongated thin rule (`—>`). Hover state triggers a subtle 4px lateral shift with a vermilion (`#D14D28`) color transition.
- **Audio / Spatial Controls:** Minimalist square cells (36px x 36px) with razor hairline borders, housing precise geometric glyphs (play, mute, orbit).

### Project & Archive Cards (World Portals)
- Never use standard boxed cards with shadows.
- Projects are presented as **Curatorial Plates**: an unbordered, high-resolution media viewport spanning varying grid columns, accompanied underneath by a clean single-line metadata rule.
- Metadata line: Index number (e.g., `WORLDS // 004`), Project Title in `headline-sm`, Year, and Medium (e.g., *Spatial Projection / Soundscape* in `meta-technical`).
- Image transitions reveal curatorial excerpts or slow-motion video loops without transforming card scale.

### Curatorial Chips & Status Indicators
- Rectangular micro-labels enclosed in a 1px stone hairline (`#8C867E` at 30% opacity).
- Displayed in uppercase `label-caps` with wide tracking.
- Live exhibition or active status is indicated by an un-rounded 4x4px vermilion square dot positioned adjacent to the status string.

### Form Fields & Inquiries
- Underline-only inputs: A singular 1px baseline border in `#8C867E` that transitions to `#161514` upon focus.
- Labels sit permanently above the field in `meta-technical`, eliminating floating placeholder animations in favor of classic print layout discipline.
- Checkboxes and radios are constructed as crisp, sharp squares. Radio selects display an inner solid square rather than a circle.

### Curatorial Index / Project Lists
- Full-width rows separated by 1px hairline rules.
- Columnar layout: `[01] Index` (Space Grotesk) | `Title` (Bodoni Moda) | `Discipline / Role` (Manrope) | `Location / Year` (Space Grotesk).
- Hovering over an index row surfaces an anchored or cursor-following floating media preview viewport without disrupting layout flow.

### Media Viewports & Full-Bleed Projections
- Dedicated container elements that break the standard column margin to touch screen edges, framed by top and bottom technical index bars detailing focal length, runtime, and acoustic channel configuration.
